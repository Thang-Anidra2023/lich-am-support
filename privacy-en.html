Privacy Policy

Last updated: 21 September 2026

Summary

Wota – Vietnamese Lunar Calendar does not track users, display advertisements, or collect or sell personal data.

Data on your device

Settings and personal events you create are stored on your device. This data may be included in backups managed by your Apple account and operating system.

Notifications

Reminders are handled through Apple’s notification system. You can change or disable notification permissions at any time in iOS Settings.

In-app support purchases

If you choose to support the app, the transaction is processed by Apple through the App Store. The developer does not receive your payment card details.

Third-party services

The app does not integrate third-party advertising, behavioural analytics, or tracking technologies.
